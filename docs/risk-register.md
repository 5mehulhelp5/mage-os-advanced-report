# Risk register

Single source of truth for the known risks of the reporting semantic layer. Seeded from the generated
[metric inventory](metric-inventory.md) (fan-out sources), the README "Known limits" and the p1a discovery findings.
Owner sub-plans: p1b (correctness tests), p1c (security and cache context), p1d (timezone and currency), p1e (cache freshness), p1f (cache correctness), p1g (release: documentation and limits).

| ID | Area | Risk | Affected metrics/datasets | Severity | Owner sub-plan | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Fan-out | `category_id` is a fan-out dimension (a product in several categories appears in each, join `category` is many): totals may not equal the sum of the rows (README "category_id non-additive" limit; a warning is shown). | `sales_items` x `category_id`: all additive metrics (`units_sold`, `units_refunded`, `units_shipped`, `units_to_ship`, `gross_sales`, `discounts`, `returns`, `net_sales`, `cost`, `gross_margin`, `margin_pct`, `return_rate`, `shipments`, `credit_memos`, `cost_coverage`, hidden `has_cost_rows`, `rows`). `orders` (count_distinct) is not flagged. No metric uses a fan-out join. | medium | p1b | open, warning shown |
| R02 | Fan-out | No fan-out join (cardinality `many`) is used by any metric in the shipped definitions; the `child` join is `one`. Re-check with the inventory when definitions change. | all datasets | low | p1b | none today |
| R03 | Verification | Expression providers (`first_order`, `first_shipment_wait`, `shipment_count`, `credit_memo_count`) are reported as `provider:<name>` and their subqueries are not analysed by the inventory; verify by integration test. | `sales_orders.new_customer_orders`, `customer_orders.avg_wait_days`, `sales_items.shipments`, `sales_items.credit_memos` | medium | p1b | open |
| R04 | Attribution | Returns are attributed to the order date, not the credit memo date; past periods change on refund (closed periods cached 24h). | `returns`, `net_sales`, `units_refunded`, `return_rate` (all datasets) | medium | p1g | documented |
| R05 | Attribution | Units shipped and to ship are attributed to the order date: `units_to_ship` is the backlog of orders of the period, past periods change as shipments are created; bundles shipped separately count 0 shipped and 0 to ship on the bundle line. | `units_shipped`, `units_to_ship`, `shipments` (`sales_items`) | medium | p1g | documented |
| R06 | Product types | Bundle products count as a single line in `sales_items` (parent row; children not reported separately). | `sales_items` metrics | low | p1g | documented |
| R07 | Dimensions | A multiselect attribute used as dimension is bucketed by its raw stored value (for example `12,15`), not split per option. | `sales_items` and `catalog_stock` attribute dimensions | low | p1g | documented |
| R08 | Customers | New vs returning analysis accepts a report range of at most 12 months on MySQL (`RequestValidator::FIRST_ORDER_MAX_MONTHS`, a maximum range, not a lookback window: the first-order lookup checks all earlier counted orders); a guest checkout later followed by a registered account for the same person is counted as new twice. | `new_customer_orders`, `returning_customer_orders` (`sales_orders`) | medium | p1b | documented |
| R09 | Dimensions | A gender value without a matching option (legacy or removed option id) is shown raw. | `customers` gender dimension | low | p1g | documented |
| R10 | Inventory | Stock value uses the global (default scope) product price and cost, not website or store overrides. | `catalog_stock` stock value metrics | medium | p1g | documented |
| R11 | Compatibility | `Magento_Reports` is a hard dependency (parent admin menu entry). | module | low | p1g | documented |
| R12 | Compatibility | Magento Open Source / Mage-OS only: EAV joins use `entity_id`, Adobe Commerce `row_id` (staging) is unsupported. | EAV-based dimensions, all datasets | medium | p1g | documented |
| R13 | Currency | When selected stores use different base currencies, amounts are summed without conversion and only a warning is shown. Resolved: monetary requests over several base currencies (configuration and data probe, main and comparison period) are rejected with HTTP 400 and the warning was removed; see [time and currency](time-and-currency.md#rejection-of-mixed-base-currencies). | all currency metrics (`gross_sales`, `discounts`, `returns`, `net_sales`, `shipping`, `taxes`, `adjustments`, `total_sales`, `aov`, `cost`, ...) | critical | p1d | mitigated: rejection (closed) |
| R14 | Customers | A customer who changes email is split into two customers. | `customer_orders.unique_customers`, customer dimensions | medium | p1g | documented |
| R15 | Customers | Customer accounts with `store_id` 0 (admin-created) or NULL (deleted store) are excluded when specific stores are selected, and from "All stores" when stores span several timezones; counted in a single-timezone install. | `customers` dataset, customer analysis | medium | p1b | documented |
| R16 | Performance | Large customer lists are capped by `max_rows`; results are truncated with a warning. | `customers`, `customer_orders` | low | p1g | documented |
| R17 | Timezone | Without database timezone tables the module groups by UTC slots (60 minutes when all offsets in scope are whole-hour, 15 minutes otherwise) and re-aggregates in PHP: results are exact for every timezone. Only non-mergeable metrics (`avg_wait_days`, `unique_customers`) come back empty with a warning when grouped by order date; the absolute row cap `max_rows * 50` is kept, so non whole-hour timezones reach the truncation warning sooner. See [time and currency](time-and-currency.md#fallback-without-database-timezone-tables). | `customer_orders.avg_wait_days`, `customer_orders.unique_customers` | medium | p1d | mitigated: fallback exact, documented |
| R18 | Cache | `days_since_last_order` is relative to today, so cached closed periods (24h TTL) may be up to one day stale. | `customers.days_since_last_order` | low | p1e | documented |
| R19 | Security | Cache key lacks the admin ACL context: users with different permissions may share cached results. | all datasets (cache) | high | p1c | mitigated: `AclContext` fingerprint in the cache key (see [security](security.md)) |
| R20 | Security | PII dimensions were gated only by the dataset ACL. Revised decision: each dataset also requires the core ACL of its entity (`DatasetAccess`). Personal data in order datasets follows `Magento_Sales::sales_order`, as in the core order view; `customers` requires `Magento_Customer::manage`. | `customers`, `customer_orders`, `sales_orders`, `sales_items` | high | p1c | mitigated, see [security](security.md) |
| R21 | Cache | Cache freshness is TTL-only (no invalidation on order/refund events); documented by decision. | all datasets (cache) | low | p1e | accepted, documented in [cache](cache.md) (key, TTLs, freshness per report category); proven by integration tests (p1f) |
| R22 | Testing | Integration coverage (`Test/Integration`, Magento integration framework, hand-computed values) now covers statuses, monetary components, refunds, derived ratios and empty periods, date/store filters, product types, customers, physical and salable stock (p1b). Metrics without a value assertion are listed in R24; non-default stocks in R26. See [metric definitions](metric-definitions.md). | all datasets | medium | p1b | mostly mitigated, remaining gaps R24-R26 |
| R23 | Compatibility | Magento 2.4.7 compatibility is unverified: 2.4.7-p10 is out of the CI matrix because Composer blocks its league/flysystem 2.x (advisory PKSA-w9tt-7782-78jx) and no fixed 2.x exists. | module on 2.4.7-p10 / PHP 8.3 | medium | p1b | open |
| R24 | Testing | Metrics run by integration tests (no SQL error, empty-period values) but without a hand-computed value assertion: `units_shipped`, `units_to_ship`, `shipments`, `credit_memos`, `avg_wait_days`, `avg_shipping_cost`, `skus_in_stock`, `skus_salable`, preset metrics `units_sold_30d`, `avg_daily_sales`, `coverage_days`, `days_since_last_sale`. R03 (expression providers `shipment_count`, `credit_memo_count`, `first_shipment_wait`) stays open for the same reason. Shipment-based tests need shipment fixtures. | `sales_items`, `customer_orders`, `catalog_stock`, `catalog_salable` | medium | p1g | open |
| R25 | Testing | The Magento integration framework of this version honours only method-level `#[Config]`; class-level `#[Config]` (and in some cases class-level `#[DataFixture]`) is ignored and method-level fixtures replace class-level ones. The class-level `#[Config]` in the p1a `OrderScenarioTest` is therefore a no-op (defaults happened to match); new tests pin config per method. Also, the integration environment charges flat-rate shipping 5.00 per ordered unit even with carrier type `O`. | integration tests | low | p1g | open, worked around |
| R26 | Inventory | Salable, reserved and stock quantities for non-default stocks (multi-stock, lazily created `inventory_stock_<id>` tables) are covered only by unit tests (`SalableStockSelectProviderTest`), not by integration tests (DDL inside the test transaction leaks). Review Decision of p1b. | `catalog_salable` (`stock_qty`, `reserved_qty`, `salable_qty`) | medium | p1g | open, unit-test only |
| R27 | Inventory | Without MSI there is no `catalog_salable` dataset, no reserved quantity and no source dimension (legacy provider); dependent presets are dropped. A source item with status 0 still counts its physical quantity in `qty_on_hand`. | `catalog_stock`, `catalog_salable` | low | p1g | documented |
| R28 | Customers | `new_customer_orders` counts a guest later registered as new twice (R08) and a customer changing email as two customers (R14); a canceled first order is ignored when deciding new vs returning. Verified by integration tests, semantics unchanged. | `new_customer_orders`, `returning_customer_orders` | low | p1g | documented |
| R29 | Security | Roles that only have a module dataset ACL lose the datasets after the upgrade until the matching core ACL is granted. | all datasets | medium | p1c | accepted, documented |
| R30 | Security | No website-scoped admin roles in Open Source: an admin allowed to read a dataset sees all stores. | all datasets | medium | p1c | accepted, documented |
| R31 | Security | An owner who loses the core permission of a dataset no longer sees nor can delete their saved reports on it (review decision). | saved reports | low | p1c | accepted |
| R32 | Security | `advanced-report:explain` with a preset query (`LowStockCoverageQuery`) runs an inner report with ACL checks and no admin session; CLI commands are trusted (shell access, no ACL). | CLI | low | p1c | accepted, pre-existing |
| R33 | Deployment | Stale `generated/code/MageOS/AdvancedReport` interceptors break the controllers after upgrade (constructors changed): run `setup:di:compile` (production) or clear generated code (developer mode). | module | low | p1c | documented |
| R34 | Timezone | On the fall back DST day the repeated hour is merged into one hourly bucket; hourly comparison across a DST day may leave the comparison's extra repeated-hour bucket without a current-period row (totals unaffected). | hourly reports | low | p1d | documented |
| R35 | Timezone | A scope spanning several timezones buckets each store in its own local time (the scope timezone and first day come from the default config scope). | all dated datasets | low | p1d | documented |
| R36 | Currency | Monetary reports for "All stores" (or any selection) are rejected on installs with several base currencies; no conversion exists. | monetary metrics of `sales_orders`, `customer_orders`, `sales_items` | medium | p1d | accepted, documented |

| R38 | Performance | Pagination limits the rows returned, not the database work: the grouped aggregation still reads every row of the period (and runs again as a count when the page is full); date bucket requests are unpaged. | R42 | Deployment | Building the module indexes on very large `sales_order` / `sales_order_item` tables takes time and I/O during `setup:upgrade` (online, `ALGORITHM=INPLACE, LOCK=NONE`, but not free). Measure with `dev/benchmark/index-timing.sql`; see [indexes](indexes.md#installation-time-and-maintenance-window). | `sales_order`, `sales_order_item` | medium | p1e | measured (see [indexes](indexes.md#installation-time-and-maintenance-window)) |
